# Nakshatra photography request

The first swap needs about **55 selected images**, not all 1,000+. Please send original-resolution files (ideally 3000 px or wider, no WhatsApp compression, no text/watermarks). RAW/JPEG/HEIC are all workable.

## Priority 1 — launch essentials (20 images)

1. **Homepage hero** — 3 wide landscape photos, 16:9
   - Best exterior or pool-at-dusk view
   - Clean architecture, warm lights on, no crowd
   - Leave some darker/quiet space on the left for headline text
2. **Pool** — 3 photos
   - Wide infinity-edge view at golden hour
   - Daytime family-safe view
   - Indoor/children’s pool
3. **Rooms** — 12 photos, two per category
   - Classic, Deluxe, Superior, Luxury, Suite, Family
   - For each: one wide image from the doorway/corner + one detail/alternate angle
   - Beds perfectly made; curtains open; all lights on; no people or personal items
4. **Restaurant** — 2 photos
   - One wide ambience shot
   - One beautifully plated signature dish or dining-table detail

## Priority 2 — conversion pages (20 images)

5. **Weddings & events** — 6 photos
   - Banquet hall empty and fully dressed
   - Lawn ceremony/setup
   - Reception/night lighting
   - Stage/decor close detail
   - One real celebration wide shot (only if guest usage rights are cleared)
   - One food/service moment
6. **Property & amenities** — 8 photos
   - Lobby/reception, exterior entrance, lawns, kids’ play area, outdoor sports, conference room, parking/arrival, breakfast
7. **Room detail galleries** — 6 photos
   - Balcony/view, bathroom, dressing area, suite living area, family multi-bed layout, in-room work area

## Priority 3 — brand story & gallery (15 images)

8. **Lifestyle details** — 5 photos
   - Welcome/arrival detail, linens, breakfast pour, evening lights, garden detail
9. **Dining** — 5 photos
   - Buffet, vegetarian main course, breakfast, dessert, restaurant service
10. **Celebrations** — 5 photos
   - Birthday, conference, poolside event, intimate party, lawn gathering

## File naming

Use folders or filenames like:

- `01-hero/hero-pool-dusk-01.jpg`
- `02-rooms/classic-wide-01.jpg`
- `02-rooms/classic-detail-02.jpg`
- `03-wedding/banquet-wide-01.jpg`
- `04-dining/restaurant-wide-01.jpg`

## Please also include

- The current restaurant menu with item names and prices
- Approved room rates, occupancy, bed configuration and room size for each category
- Approved guest reviews with guest name/source
- Confirmed Facebook, YouTube and Tripadvisor URLs
- Check-in/check-out, deposit and cancellation rules approved by the hotel

The live site keeps each image container’s dimensions fixed, so these replacements will not shift the layout.
